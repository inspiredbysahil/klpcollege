# K.L.P. College programme comparison and official-site coverage

## What students will get

- A dedicated **Compare programmes** page, reachable from Academics and each programme page. Students can select programmes and compare eligibility, duration, study level, and clear links to admission information side by side; it will remain usable on phones.
- An expanded navigation reflecting the **major sections** of the official college site: About, Students, Academics, Faculty, Gallery, Events & Activities, News & Notices, IQAC, Alumni, and Contact. Group related destinations in accessible desktop dropdowns and expandable mobile menus rather than reproducing the official site's very long menus verbatim.
- Useful destination pages for those major sections, with concise, independently written summaries of verified information and prominent links to the corresponding official pages, documents, portals, and updates. Preserve the existing site's visual identity and working routes.

## Content and accuracy

- Audit each relevant official page and its links before publishing facts. Keep the programme list and other public facts centralized so comparison rows and detail pages stay consistent.
- Show a numerical duration or specific eligibility **only when the source clearly identifies that exact programme**. Some official course material is older or mixed (for example, the B.Com page discusses B.Com Hons.), so label historical criteria and session dates clearly rather than presenting them as current admission rules. Where current details cannot be verified, show “Confirm with admissions” with a direct official link instead of guessing.
- Link directly to the official 2026–27 admission page and its application destination where appropriate. Summarize original material rather than copying the entire official site, and point visitors to the original for complete policies, notices, and records.

## Technical approach

- Extend the shared college data with per-programme source URLs and verified comparison fields; add a shareable comparison route and selection controls with clear empty and maximum-selection states.
- Add route files and distinct sharing metadata for each new major section. Extend the shared navigation for keyboard, pointer, touch, and mobile use, keeping existing student-service shortcuts.
- Check links, comparison interactions, navigation, route metadata, image behavior, and layout at desktop and mobile sizes; resolve any errors found.
